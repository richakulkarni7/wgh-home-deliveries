Key findings
Summary insights from narrative analysis of all 25 weeks
Highest concern
Dadenggre CHC — recorded 0% ID in multiple weeks; highest HD rate among CHCs at 38–67% in several weeks. Persistent refusal culture and inaccessible sub-centre areas are the primary drivers.
Systemic issue
Failure to inform ASHA is the single largest category (~198 instances). Even willing mothers deliver at home because the communication chain breaks down at the moment of labour.
Consistent performers
Bhaitbari CHC, Selsella CHC, Jeldupara PHC, Pedaldoba PHC — regularly achieve 90–100% ID rates. Bhaitbari CHC is the highest-volume facility in the district and maintains strong performance.
Infrastructure gap
Hard-to-reach areas + poor network together account for ~154 mentions. These are concentrated in Allagre, Chibinang, Darengre, and parts of Asanang — requiring targeted transport and communication solutions.
