Key findings
Insights from narrative analysis of all 26 weeks
Highest concern
Dadenggre CHC — recorded 0% ID in multiple weeks. Persistent refusal culture and inaccessible sub-centres are the primary drivers.
Systemic issue
Failure to inform ASHA — ~198 instances. Even willing mothers deliver at home when the communication chain breaks at the moment of labour.
Consistent performers
Bhaitbari CHC, Selsella CHC, Jeldupara PHC, Pedaldoba PHC — regularly achieve 90–100% ID rates.
Infrastructure gap
Hard-to-reach areas + poor network — ~154 combined mentions. Concentrated in Allagre, Darengre, Chibinang, and parts of Asanang.
